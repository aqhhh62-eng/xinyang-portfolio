Field notes.

Ten landscapes photographed between first light and last light. Nothing staged, nothing added — only weather, distance and the time it takes to stand still.

  1. 01

    Still Water Lake · Dawn

    Fog resting on the surface before the light arrives; the water reads like paper.

  2. 02

    Pines Forest · Fog

    A stand of firs holding mist between the trunks — texture without detail.

  3. 03

    Dune Desert · Dusk

    Wind draws one long curve; almost no shadow, almost no sense of scale.

  4. 04

    Coastline Atlantic · Blue hour

    A long exposure flattens the sea into a single soft band of blue.

  5. 05

    Ridges Prealps · Sunrise

    Layer after layer of hills, each one paler than the one in front of it.

  6. 06

    Field Snow · Winter

    One bare tree in an empty field: the quietest composition winter offers.

  7. 07

    Open Sea North Sea · Noon

    Water and sky meet in the same colour; the horizon almost disappears.

  8. 08

    Birch Forest · Haze

    White trunks dissolving into white air — closer to drawing than photography.

  9. 09

    Shoreline Aerial · Midday

    Foam traces a thin line where pale sand meets pale water.

  10. 10

    Dusk Mountains · Evening

    Ridges fold into violet as the light leaves the valley.
